Hypothesis Testing: Statistical Validation

Chi-Square Test (Cancellation vs Hotel Type)

• χ² Statistic = 2224.9 (df = 1)
• p-value < 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁶ (p < 0.001)
• Decision: Reject H₀. Cancellation rate is strongly associated with hotel type.

One-Way ANOVA (ADR by Hotel Type)

• F-Statistic = 1127 (df = 1, 119388)
• p-value < 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁶ (p < 0.001)
• Decision: Reject H₀. Mean ADR differs significantly across property types.

Data Storytelling: Core Operational Narrative

High cancellation rates (37.04%) erode hotel revenue efficiency. Long lead times (>90 days) paired with zero-deposit policies account for the majority of unfulfilled bookings. Revenue managers must dynamically tie cancellation policies to booking lead times.

Slide 9 of 9

Actionable Revenue Recommendations

Deposit & Policy Shift

• Mandate non-refundable deposits for bookings made >90 days in advance.
• Implement early-bird pricing tiers with strict cancellation terms.

Channel Strategy

• Incentivize Direct and Corporate booking channels which display higher completion rates than third-party OTAs.
