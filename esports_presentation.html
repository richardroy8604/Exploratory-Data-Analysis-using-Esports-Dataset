Hypothesis Testing: One-Way ANOVA

ANOVA Formalization

H₀: μ_MOBA = μ_FPS = μ_Strategy = ... (Mean log prize earnings are equal across genres)
H₁: At least one genre has significantly different mean earnings.
α = 0.05

Statistical Test Results

• F-Statistic = 6.807 (df = 8, 630)
• p-value = 1.36 × 10⁻⁸ (p < 0.001)
• Decision: Reject H₀. Prize money differs significantly across genres.

Data Storytelling: Core Narrative

The competitive esports economy functions under a winner-take-all ecosystem. While headline figures like Dota 2's $360M attract public attention, 50% of esports titles earn under $39,280 over their lifetime. Sustained monetization depends on maintaining physical LAN finals and fostering community tournament infrastructure.
